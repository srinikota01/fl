Programs

Ages 2 – 3

Little Thinkers

Gentle sensory play, stories and movement that help little ones notice, name and navigate their world.

Ages 3 – 4

Curious Explorers

Theme-based investigations in nature, sound and pattern — building early language, number sense and social skills.

Ages 4 – 5

Early Innovators

Simple build-and-test challenges that introduce observation, early STEM thinking and joyful problem solving.
